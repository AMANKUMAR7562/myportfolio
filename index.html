Frequently Asked Questions

Questions & Answers

Here are answers to some of the most common questions I receive as a UI/UX & product designer. If you don’t see your question here, feel free to reach out directly!

Ask a Question ↗
I specialize in comprehensive UI/UX design for web and mobile applications, end-to-end design systems creation, interactive prototyping, brand identity design, and high-performance frontend visual architecture.
My process follows four structured phases: 1. Discovery & research (understanding users, business goals, and technical boundaries), 2. Information architecture & wireframes, 3. High-fidelity visual design & interactive prototypes, and 4. Design tokens and developer handoff documentation.
Timelines depend on scope. A targeted design sprint or high-converting landing page typically takes 1–2 weeks. Comprehensive product redesigns, full mobile applications, and enterprise design systems typically span 4–8 weeks with iterative milestone reviews.
You receive clean, production-ready Figma source files with Auto-Layout v5 components, responsive layouts, design token libraries, clickable interactive prototypes, and exported high-resolution assets ready for engineering implementation.
Having a background in Computer Science (B.Tech CSE), I speak developers' language. I organize Figma files with design tokens, responsive breakpoints, state variants, and exact CSS property specifications so developers can build with zero ambiguity.
