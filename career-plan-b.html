01 // Strategic Foundation

From Information Repository to Decision Platform

Career Plan B is an established career guidance and education platform in India helping students make informed, high-stakes decisions about careers, colleges, courses, and entrance examinations.

The Legacy Experience

Dense, Fragmented & Content-Heavy

Like many information-heavy counseling platforms, the previous iteration accumulated rich data over years but presented it through unstructured article pages and disconnected links. Users had to do the heavy lifting of synthesizing information themselves.

  • ✕
    Cognitive Overload: Students encountered assessments, college stats, exams, and counseling resources scattered with no progressive hierarchy.
  • ✕
    Unclear User Journeys: Visitors were presented with text walls rather than guided funnels directing them toward their next meaningful milestone.
  • ✕
    Institutional Stiffness: Dense layouts made critical life decisions feel stressful and bureaucratic rather than empowering and approachable.
→
The Product-Led Response

Structured, Trustworthy & Action-Oriented

The redesign transformed Career Plan B into a structured digital product. We treated complex admission rules, medical service bonds, and fee structures as interactive tools that give users immediate clarity.

  • ✓
    Intent-Based Navigation: Content organized around user intent: Discover → Understand → Explore → Evaluate → Take Action.
  • ✓
    Interactive Product Tools: Dedicated interactive modules (MBBS Bond Explorer, True Cost Calculator, College Predictor) instead of static tables.
  • ✓
    Dual-Audience Trust: Visual architecture engineered to inspire confidence in parents while feeling vibrant and approachable for students.
GOAL 01

Intent-Driven Paths

Shift from an unstructured article library into guided, step-by-step user funnels: Discover → Understand → Explore → Evaluate → Take Action.

GOAL 02

Interactive Tool Engines

Convert dense state gazettes, bonds, and fees into interactive tools (MBBS Bond Explorer, True Cost Calculator, College Predictor).

GOAL 03

Dual-Stakeholder Trust

Balance vibrant, accessible visual discovery for students with authoritative financial and institutional transparency for parents.

GOAL 04

Frictionless Mentorship

Bridge digital self-service tools with direct, confidence-building booking pathways to accredited career counselors.

02 // Problem Framing

The Three Structural Friction Points

Career decisions are among the most stressful, high-consideration crossroads in a student's life. The redesign addressed three fundamental friction points identified across user interviews and behavioral analytics.

01 Too Much Information

Students were bombarded with career assessments, college admission criteria, entrance exam calendars, and counseling brochures across siloed sections, creating paralysis by analysis.

02 Unclear User Journeys

Users needed proactive guidance on what to do next based on their academic score and aspirations, rather than simply reading passive encyclopedia-style content.

03 Trust & Credibility

Because educational decisions involve major financial investments and multi-year commitments, the interface needed to project authoritative credibility without feeling outdated.

03 // User Research & Synthesis

Dual-Stakeholder Ecosystem

Educational platforms must balance two deeply intertwined personas: the ambitious student navigating academic identity, and the pragmatic parent evaluating investment and security.

ST

The Exploring Student

Primary User · High School & Undergraduate Aspirants
Primary Needs & Explorations

Career specializations, college admissions odds, entrance exam countdowns, eligibility requirements, and personalized psychometric guidance.

Core Frustrations

Overwhelmed by confusing government quota rules, conflicting forum advice, and lack of visual clarity on true educational career pathways.

Design Solution

Scannable card clusters, gamified interactive calculators, visual timelines, and frictionless booking for one-on-one professional mentorship.

PR

The Evaluating Parent

Secondary User · Decision Sponsor & Financial Guarantor
Primary Needs & Explorations

Authentic institutional accreditation, true financial commitments beyond sticker tuition, mandatory rural service bond liabilities, and verified ROI.

Core Frustrations

Skeptical of opaque commercial counseling agencies, hidden fees in medical degrees, and unclear long-term licensing restrictions.

Design Solution

Rigorous financial breakdown engines (True Cost Calculator), clear statutory bond disclosure tools, and prominent trust indicators highlighting 15+ years of verified heritage.

04 // Systematic Architecture

Reorganizing Around User Intent

Instead of treating every feature as an isolated page, the redesign reorganized the entire ecosystem into four clear intent pillars, shifting Career Plan B from an information repository into an actionable decision system.

01. Career Discovery

Understand yourself → Explore career paths → Compare options. Self-assessment engines and guided specialization trees designed to spark clarity.

02. College & Admissions

Discover colleges → Understand admissions → Explore eligibility. Centralized directory of verified institutions with cutoffs and quota breakdowns.

03. Exams & Opportunities

Find relevant exams → Track dates → Understand processes. Real-time examination calendar with countdown trackers and syllabus prerequisites.

04. Tools & Calculators

Calculate → Compare → Evaluate. Interactive decision engines that synthesize dense financial, bond, and merit data into immediate answers.

Design Evolution: Before vs. After

Before Redesign
  • Information-First: Exhaustive text dumps that required users to read thousands of words to extract answers.
  • Dense Layouts: Cluttered pages with competing font styles, generic imagery, and no clear entry focal point.
  • Weak Hierarchy: Tools like the bond calculator were hidden deep within nested menu levels.
  • Content-Led Navigation: Pages linked arbitrarily without a guided decision pathway.
  • Limited Interaction: Static HTML tables that failed to engage the digital-native student.
After Redesign
  • Intent-Driven Navigation: Direct entry points tailored to what the user wants to achieve today.
  • Clear Visual Hierarchy: High-impact typography, generous spacing, and modular bento card structures.
  • Product-Led Experience: Proprietary calculators positioned prominently as core hero products.
  • Stronger Storytelling: Transparent editorial narratives combined with responsive interactive graphs.
  • Action-Oriented CTAs: Direct bridges connecting tool insights with certified human counseling.

08 // Design Methodology

My Role & End-to-End Ownership

As the Product & UI/UX Designer, I led the complete redesign lifecycle from exploratory user research to high-fidelity interactive design systems and developer collaboration.

01

UX Research & Problem Framing

Conducted in-depth stakeholder and user interviews with high school students, parents, and senior career counselors to map pain points in the existing information architecture.

02

Information Architecture & User-Flow Definition

Re-architected hundreds of legacy content nodes into four intent pillars (Career Discovery, College Admissions, Exams, and Decision Tools), establishing intuitive multi-step exploration funnels.

03

Wireframing & Rapid Iteration

Created low- and mid-fidelity wireframes in FigJam and Figma to test layout hierarchy, scannability, and progressive disclosure mechanisms across desktop and mobile form factors.

04

UI Design & Design System Development

Engineered a comprehensive design system in Figma featuring academic navy, growth green, and warm yellow accents, paired with modular bento cards, responsive tables, and accessible type tokens.

05

Interactive Tool Interfaces & Calculators

Designed the dedicated interfaces for the MBBS Bond Explorer, True Cost Calculator, and College Predictor, translating complex legal gazettes and algorithms into intuitive sliders and visual cards.

06

Responsive Optimization & High-Fidelity Prototyping

Built fully responsive layouts and micro-animated prototypes to validate touch targets, mobile navigation, and seamless developer hand-off for live production rollout.
